## How to quote
- In cold emails and first conversations, say "**starting from**" the lower price (e.g. "websites starting from ₹6,000"). Never quote the top of a range as a fixed price.
- The exact price comes only after understanding the scope. The account-manager builds the quote within these ranges. Anything outside a range, any discount and any package deal needs the owner's approval.
- Always make clear that the ad management fee does **not** include the ad spend.
