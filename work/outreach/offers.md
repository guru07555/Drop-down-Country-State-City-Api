## Google Branding package (from ₹20,000 / month)
Goal: when someone searches the business name on Google, the first page shows the business's own website, profiles, articles, images and videos, and the business looks trustworthy.

**Included (owner-defined):**
1. **Website:** a new website or optimization of the existing one, with brand SEO, schema markup (Organization / LocalBusiness) and an About page that builds trust.
2. **Social media profiles:** set up, brand and optimize Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
3. **Articles & blogs:** published on the client's website and on relevant third-party platforms.
4. **Press releases:** written and published about the brand. [If a paid distribution platform is used, whether that cost is included or extra: owner to confirm.]
5. **Images:** branded, keyword-named, optimized images so the brand shows up in Google Images.
6. **YouTube:** channel setup and branding, with brand videos so they show up in Google's video results.
7. **Instagram & Facebook:** regular branded posting so these profiles rank for the brand name.

**Also included (team's suggested additions; owner can remove any):**
8. **Google Business Profile:** setup or verification, full optimization, photos, weekly posts, Q&A and a review-request system (genuine customer reviews only).
9. **LinkedIn company page** (plus Pinterest / X where relevant to the business).
10. **Business listings / citations:** Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha and industry directories, with consistent name, address and phone (NAP).
11. **Quora / community answers:** helpful, honest answers that mention the brand where relevant.
12. **Brand search monitoring:** a monthly report with screenshots of the brand-name Google results (what ranks on page 1 now vs. last month), plus suppression of irrelevant or outdated results by building stronger positive pages.
13. **Knowledge-panel-ready setup:** consistent profiles, schema and sameAs links that help Google understand the brand.

**Not included:** Wikipedia pages (excluded by the owner). Also never: fake reviews, paid links/PBNs or fake news sites.

**How to sell it honestly:** Google decides what ranks and whether to show a knowledge panel. Promise the work and a steadily stronger brand search page, never "guaranteed #1" or a "guaranteed knowledge panel". Results usually build over 3–6 months.

## How to quote
- In cold emails and first conversations, say "**starting from**" the lower price (e.g. "websites starting from ₹6,000"). Never quote the top of a range as a fixed price.
- The exact price comes only after understanding the scope. The account-manager builds the quote within these ranges. Anything outside a range, any discount and any package deal needs the owner's approval.
- Always make clear that the ad management fee does **not** include the ad spend.
