Golden rules for the counsellor
- Pehle suno, phir batao. Student ke goal ke hisaab se course batao, pressure mat do.
- Say "100% job assistance" (resume, interview preparation, hiring partners ka access). Never promise a job ("pakki job" mat kahein). Do not quote any percentage or salary.
- Only 3-month and 6-month courses are sold. Always present Digital Marketing 6 Months first, then 3 Months.
- All fees are "+ taxes". Say it every time you quote a number.
- Classes are offline only at Ladwa. Rolling admission: kisi bhi din join kar sakte hain. Slots: 11 AM-1 PM or 4 PM-6 PM.
- Under 18: fee and enrollment ki baat parent/guardian se. Collect minimum data only.
- If they say "mat bhejo / don't contact", mark `Do not contact` immediately.

## Step 0. Before the call (1 minute)

## Step 1. Greeting (1 minute)
Call:
> "Namaste {Name} ji, main {Counsellor} bol rahi/raha hoon, Divy Digital Hub, Ladwa se. Aapne hamare yahan digital marketing course ke baare mein enquiry ki thi. Kya abhi 10-15 minute baat karne ka sahi time hai?"

If no: ask a convenient time, note it in `next_followup`.

Walk-in:
> "Namaste, aaiye baithiye. Chai/paani? Main aapko 10-15 minute mein bata dungi/dunga ki kaunsa course aapke liye sahi rahega. Ye counselling bilkul free hai, koi obligation nahi."

Consent line (for the record):
> "Main aapka naam, number aur kuch basic details apne register mein likhungi/likhunga, sirf course ki jaankari bhejne ke liye. Theek hai?"

## Step 2. Discovery questions (4-5 minutes)
Ask in this order. Write answers in the notes as you go.

1. Name, town, age group
   > "Aapka poora naam aur aap kis town se hain? Age kitni hai, roughly?"
   - **If under 18:** see "Under-18 branch" at the end of this script. Do not discuss fees or enrollment with the minor alone.
2. Education / current status
   > "Abhi aap kya kar rahe hain: padhai, graduation ke baad job ki talaash, job, apna business, ya ghar sambhalte hain?"
3. Goal (most important)
   > "Is course se aap kya chahte hain: job, freelancing, apne business ko grow karna, side income, ya skill upgrade?"
4. Current level
   > "Computer/mobile kitna chala lete hain? Social media, Canva, Excel, ya koi marketing/coding ka experience?" (Course zero se shuru hota hai, so "kuch nahi aata" is fine.)
5. Time available
   > "Din mein roz 2 ghante nikal sakte hain? Aur kaunsa slot theek rahega: subah 11 se 1 ya shaam 4 se 6?"
6. Travel to Ladwa (offline only)
   > "Hamari classes sirf Ladwa centre mein offline hoti hain, online nahi. Aap kahan se aayenge, aana-jaana roz ho payega?" (Be upfront for Kurukshetra, Yamunanagar, Pipli, Radaur, Shahabad etc.)
7. Laptop
   > "Aapke paas laptop hai? Practical kaam ke liye kabhi laptop chahiye hota hai." If no: note it, ask what they can arrange; do not promise anything not in `courses.md`. Say: "Is baare mein main confirm karke batati/batata hoon ki centre par kya facility hai."
8. Budget and decision maker
   > "Fees ke liye aapne kitna socha hai? Aur final decision aap lenge ya ghar mein kisi se poochna hoga?"
   - If decision maker is a parent/spouse: offer a joint call or ask them to visit the centre.

Summarise back in one line:
> "To main ye samjhi/samjha: aap {status} hain, {goal} chahte hain, roz {time} nikal sakte hain, {slot} slot theek hai, {town} se aayenge. Sahi?"

## Step 7. Record the result in `work/training/students.csv`
Immediately after the call (within 10 minutes). Columns:

`student_id,name,age_group,is_minor,guardian_contact_consent,town,phone,email,status_now,goal,level,preferred_mode,course_interest,source,enquiry_date,stage,next_followup,notes`

How to fill:
- `student_id`: next number, format `S-001`, `S-002`... (sample students use `SAMPLE-xx`; never mix sample rows into the real CSV).
- `age_group`: e.g. `18-22`, `23-30`, `31+`, `<18`.
- `is_minor`: `Yes` / `No`. If Yes, `guardian_contact_consent` = `Yes` only after the parent/guardian has agreed; otherwise `Pending`. For adults write `NA`.
- `phone`, `email`: only what the person gave you.
- `status_now`: student/graduate/working/business owner/homemaker.
- `goal`: job / freelancing / own business / side income / skill upgrade.
- `level`: basic / some experience / none.
- `preferred_mode`: `Offline (Ladwa)` (only mode). If travel is impossible, write in notes: "travel issue" and set stage per follow-up result.
- `course_interest`: `DM-6M`, `DM-3M`, `WD-3M`, `WD-6M`, `AD-3M`, `AD-6M`.
- `source`: form / call / WhatsApp / walk-in / seminar / referral / Google profile etc.
- `stage`: `New enquiry -> Counselled -> Plan sent -> Demo attended -> Enrolled -> Completed -> Placed/Freelancing`, or `Not interested` / `Do not contact`. After this call: `Counselled`.
- `next_followup`: date + what (e.g. `2026-10-12 day-1 plan + demo invite`).
- `notes`: slot preferred (11-1 / 4-6), laptop yes/no, travel OK yes/no, budget said, decision maker, objections raised, installment vs full-payment leaning, advance booking interest. No sensitive info beyond this.

## Under-18 branch
1. Ask age early. If under 18:
   > "Aap abhi padhai kar rahe hain, to fees aur admission ki baat hum aapke mummy/papa se bhi karenge. Kya unka number ya woh centre aa sakte hain?"
2. Collect only: name, town, class/status, goal, and the parent's/guardian's contact **with the parent's consent**. No social profiles, no photos, no ID documents at this stage.
3. Talk fees, installments and joining date with the parent/guardian only. Use the "Parent message" template.
4. Set `is_minor = Yes`, `guardian_contact_consent = Pending` until the parent agrees; `Yes` after.
5. Do not send promotional messages directly to a minor's phone once the parent's number is available; message the parent.
6. Be honest about age fit: if the child is too young to attend regularly or travel alone to Ladwa, say so and suggest waiting rather than enrolling.
