# Objection Handling: Ladwa / Kurukshetra context

Principles: listen first, agree with the worry, answer honestly, offer a next step. Quote only `courses.md`. "+ taxes" always. "100% job assistance" means resume building, interview preparation and access to hiring partners, not a promised job. No salary or percentage claims. No fake urgency. Discounts beyond the listed ones need the owner's approval.

Quick facts to keep handy
- DM 6 Months: Rs 35,000 + taxes (3 installments: Rs 11,667 + Rs 11,667 + Rs 11,666 at joining / month 2 / month 4). Full payment: Rs 30,000 + taxes.
- DM 3 Months: Rs 20,000 + taxes (Rs 6,667 + Rs 6,667 + Rs 6,666 at joining / month 2 / month 3).
- Rs 1,000 off for booking 7+ days before joining (cannot be combined with the full-payment discount).
- Centre has computers for students; laptop not required. Day 1: copy + pen. Fees: UPI or cash. Office: 9 AM–6 PM, Mon–Fri.
- Offline only at Ladwa; slots 11 AM-1 PM or 4 PM-6 PM; rolling admission.
- Free counselling and free demo class.

### 1. "Fees zyada hain"
**Meaning:** budget worry or doesn't yet see the value.
**Answer:**
> "Aapki baat sahi hai, Rs 35,000 chhoti raqam nahi. Isliye 3 equal installments ka option hai: joining par Rs 11,667, doosri month 2 mein Rs 11,667, aur teesri month 4 mein Rs 11,666 (taxes extra). Yani ek saath nahi dena padta. Aur agar ek saath de sakte hain to Rs 30,000 + taxes. Agar 6 mahine abhi bhaari lagte hain to 3 Months Rs 20,000 + taxes hai, wo bhi installments mein. Aur Rs 1,000 off milta hai agar joining se 7 din pehle seat book karein."
**Next step:** show the side-by-side table; offer free demo class so they can judge value before paying.
**Do not:** invent a discount or scholarship; say you will "check with owner" for special rates only if the student really cannot proceed, and tell them nothing is promised.

### 2. "YouTube se seekh lunga"
**Answer:**
> "YouTube par kaafi kuch milta hai, aur main uski kadar karti/karta hoon. Dikkat ye hoti hai ki wahan order nahi hota, aur aapki galtiyan koi theek nahi karta. Yahan mentor se guidance, feedback, live projects aur agency ka real client kaam seekhne ko milta hai. Plus resume, interview prep aur hiring partners tak access. Aap free demo class mein dekh lijiye ki fark kya hai."
**Next step:** demo class. Tip: ask "Aapne kab se YouTube se seekhna shuru kiya? Ab tak kya bana liya?" and listen.

### 3. "Time nahi hai"
**Answer:**
> "Roz sirf 2 ghante ka slot hai, aur do slot hain: subah 11 se 1 ya shaam 4 se 6. Aap apne time ka slot chun sakte hain. Admission kisi bhi din ho sakta hai, to padhai ke exam ya busy season ke baad bhi join ho sakte hain. Aapka course aapki joining date se gina jaata hai."
**Next step:** ask their real free hours, match a slot. If they truly cannot give daily 2 hours, say so honestly and suggest joining at a better time instead of enrolling and dropping.

### 4. "Ghar wale nahi maanenge"
**Answer:**
> "Ye bahut common baat hai, aur ghar walon ka sawaal karna bhi jayaz hai. Aap unhe saath laaiye ya unse meri baat karwa dijiye. Centre dikha denge, aur batayenge ki kya sikhaya jaata hai, fees kitni hai, installments kaise hain. Jab unhe poori jaankari hogi to unka bharosa banta hai."
**Next step:** invite parent/spouse for a centre visit or a joint call. For under-18: talk to the parent only; use the parent template.
**Do not:** push the student to hide it from family or pay without family agreement.

### 5. "Job milegi?"
**Answer (honest):**
> "Main aapse jhoot nahi bolungi/bolunga: koi bhi institute pakki job ka vaada nahi kar sakta. Hamara 100% job assistance hai: resume banwana, interview ki taiyari, aur hiring partners tak access. 6 Months plan mein month 5-6 mein internship bhi hai. Job aapki skill, portfolio aur mehnat par bhi depend karti hai. Isliye hum live projects karwate hain, taaki aapke paas dikhane ko kaam ho."
**Next step:** show the roadmap (`roadmaps/digital-marketing-6-months.md`).
**Do not:** quote percentages, salaries, or the website's old claim. Do not name students without written consent.

### 6. "Online kyun nahi? Ghar se karna hai"
**Answer:**
> "Hamari saari classes offline hoti hain, Ladwa centre mein. Online batch abhi nahi hai. Offline mein faayda ye hai ki aap trainer ke saamne kaam karte hain, doubt turant clear hota hai, aur live projects mein team ke saath practice hoti hai. Agar aap Kurukshetra, Pipli, Radaur, Shahabad ya Yamunanagar se hain to pehle sochiye aana-jaana roz ho payega. Demo class ke din aakar dekh lijiye."
**Next step:** confirm travel feasibility; pick the slot that fits transport (11-1 or 4-6).
**Do not:** promise online classes, recorded sessions or hostel/PG help (not in `courses.md`).

### 7. "Mujhe chhota (1-1.5 mahine ka) course chahiye"
**Answer:**
> "Abhi hamare yahan admissions sirf 3-month aur 6-month programs ke liye khule hain, kyunki unhi mein live projects aur job assistance milti hai. Kam samay mein sirf basics ho paate hain, kaam sikhne ke liye practice zaroori hai. Aapko jo budget ya time ki chinta hai, uske liye 3 Months Digital Marketing hai: Rs 20,000 + taxes, jo 3 equal installments (Rs 6,667 + Rs 6,667 + Rs 6,666) mein de sakte hain."
**Next step:** book a demo class.
**Do not:** quote the old short-course fee or agree to sell it, even if the website shows it.

### 8. "Doosra institute sasta hai"
**Answer:**
> "Ho sakta hai, aur aap tulna zaroor kariye. Bas ye dekhiye: kya wahan live client projects hain? Trainer kaun hai aur roz kaam karta hai ya nahi? Kya internship aur job assistance likhit mein milti hai? Kya certificate aur Google certification ki taiyari hai? Hum ek real digital agency ke saath chalte hain, isliye students ko real kaam dekhne ko milta hai. Aap dono jagah demo class lekar decide kijiye."
**Next step:** share the course outline; invite to demo class.
**Do not:** speak badly of any competitor; do not cut prices to match. Price changes need the owner's approval.

### 9. "Mere paas laptop nahi hai"
**Answer:**
> "Koi baat nahi! Hamare centre par students ke liye computers hain, to laptop ke bina bhi aap poora course kar sakte hain. Class mein practical computer par hi hota hai. Baad mein apna laptop le lein to ghar par practice aur freelancing mein madad milegi, par join karne ke liye zaroori nahi hai."
**Next step:** note "no laptop, uses centre computer" in the CSV.
**Do not:** promise EMI, a free laptop, or computer access outside class/office hours unless the owner confirms it.

### 10. "Baad mein sochunga"
**Answer:**
> "Bilkul, sochna bahut zaroori hai. Bas ye bata dijiye ki aap kis cheez par soch rahe hain: fees, time, ghar walon ki sehmati, ya course ke baare mein? Main us hisaab se jaankari bhej dungi/dunga. Admission kisi bhi din ho sakta hai, isliye koi jaldi nahi. Tab tak aap free demo class dekh lijiye, usme koi kharcha nahi."
**Next step:** fix a follow-up date (day 3 / day 7 templates).
**Do not:** create fake urgency or "offer khatam ho raha hai".

### 11. "Mujhe kuch aata hi nahi, main kar paunga?"
**Answer:**
> "Course bilkul zero se shuru hota hai aur advanced tak jaata hai, to pehle se kuch aana zaroori nahi. Bas mobile/computer chalana aana chahiye aur roz aane ki aadat. Pehle mahine mein hum basics karte hain: SEO, social media, content, Google Ads ki shuruaat."
**Next step:** demo class.

### 12. "Mera apna business hai, mere liye kya hoga?"
**Answer:**
> "Digital marketing se aap apne business ke liye khud SEO, social media, ads aur analytics samajh sakte hain, taaki kisi ek par nirbhar na rahen. 6 Months plan mein Brand Strategy, Influencer Marketing aur E-commerce Marketing bhi hai. Hum aapke apne business ko live project bana sakte hain (trainer ke saath tay karenge)."
**Next step:** ask business type, current marketing, hours; offer demo.

### 13. "Pehle se pata hai, free mein sab batao" / "Sirf information chahiye"
**Answer:**
> "Zaroor, counselling free hai. Main aapko course ki poori jaankari WhatsApp par bhej dungi/dunga. Aap jab tak chahein sochiye. Bas ek baar demo class mein aa jaaiye taaki khud dekh sakein."
**Next step:** send day-1 template; mark `Counselled`.

### 14. "Aurat/ladki hoon, akele aana-jaana mushkil hai" (safety / commute)
**Answer:**
> "Aapki chinta samajh aati hai. Aap pehle parivaar ke kisi member ke saath centre dekhne aaiye, class ka mahaul aur trainers se milaiye. Aap shaam ya subah mein jo slot suvidha-jank ho wo chun sakti hain."
**Next step:** invite family member to visit; do not promise transport.

### 15. "Certificate valid hai? Sarkari hai?"
**Answer:**
> "Course ke baad Divy Digital Hub ka completion certificate milta hai, aur Google certification ki taiyari bhi karwayi jaati hai. Ye sarkari degree nahi hai, ye skill-based training hai. Free Google, Meta aur HubSpot ke certificates aap khud online dene ka lakshya rakh sakte hain, hum taiyari mein madad karenge."
**Do not:** claim government recognition, affiliation or a certificate not written in `courses.md`.

---

## Rules for every objection
- Never pressure; never say "aaj hi bharo".
- If the person says "nahi chahiye / call mat karo", thank them and set `Do not contact`.
- If a question needs info we don't have (full-payment price of courses other than DM 6 Months), say "main confirm karke batati/batata hoon" and log it in `notes`.
