## Template do's and don'ts
- Do not add fake urgency ("sirf 2 seat bachi hai") or a made-up deadline.
- Do not quote any website percentage claim or any salary figure. Say "100% job assistance".
- Do not name a student or show a photo as a success story without written consent.
- Full-payment price of non-DM-6M courses: "call par confirm hoga".
