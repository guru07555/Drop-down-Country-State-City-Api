## Month 1: Foundation (week by week)
Topics: SEO Fundamentals, Social Media Marketing, Content Strategy Basics, Google Ads Introduction, plus AI tools and YouTube basics.

| Week | Focus | Practical task |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | How digital marketing works; SEO Fundamentals; set up your own learning Gmail, Google account and a practice social page | Make a simple keyword list for a local business idea |
| Week 2 | SEO Fundamentals continued; Social Media Marketing basics (Facebook/Instagram pages, posting) | Create and set up a practice business page |
| Week 3 | Content Strategy Basics; using AI tools for content and design | Make a 7-day content calendar with AI-assisted captions and creatives |
| Week 4 | Google Ads Introduction; start your YouTube channel | Draft a practice ad (no budget needed); upload first video; Month 1 review |

Month 1 milestones
- Practice page and content calendar ready
- First YouTube channel set up with a first video
- Start free certificate courses (Google Skillshop fundamentals, HubSpot free courses) as a goal

## Month 2: Advanced SEO and SEM
Topics: Advanced SEO and SEM (paid search), deeper Google Ads, YouTube growth.
- Practical: SEO audit of a sample website; set up and optimise a sample campaign structure.
- Milestones: SEO audit report added to portfolio; aim to complete a Google Skillshop certification (e.g., Search/Ads) and a HubSpot certificate as goals.
- Installment 2 due at start of month 2.

## Month 3: Automation, Analytics and live client projects
Topics: Email Marketing Automation, Google Analytics and Reports, 2 Live Client Projects, Google certified prep.
- Practical: build an email sequence; set up analytics and make a report; work on the 2 live client projects under trainer guidance.
- Milestones: 2 live client projects documented in your portfolio; one analytics report; Google Analytics / Skillshop certification as a goal; Meta Blueprint learning begun for social ads as a goal.

## Month 4: Brand Strategy and Influencer Marketing
Topics: Brand Strategy and Planning, Influencer Marketing.
- Practical: write a brand strategy for a real local business; plan an influencer campaign.
- Milestones: brand strategy document in portfolio; Meta Blueprint certification as a goal.
- Installment 3 due at start of month 4.

## Month 5: E-commerce Marketing and Freelancing Launchpad
Topics: E-commerce Marketing, Freelancing Launchpad, start of the internship.
- Practical: plan marketing for a sample online store; learn how to find, pitch, price and deliver freelance work; prepare service package list and portfolio page.
- Milestones: portfolio website/page ready; first freelance client or internship target set as a goal; resume builds start.

## Month 6: Internship and job assistance
Topics: Internship, 100% job assistance (resume building, interview preparation, access to hiring partners).
- Practical: internship work on real tasks; mock interviews; final portfolio review.
- Milestones: internship experience; final resume; final portfolio; interview-ready; first freelance client or job interview as a goal (goal only).

## Why months 4-6 matter versus stopping at 3
Months 1-3 teach you skills and give you 2 live client projects. Months 4-6 are where you:
1. Add Brand Strategy and Planning, Influencer Marketing and E-commerce Marketing, which businesses ask for.
2. Use the Freelancing Launchpad to learn how to find, price and handle freelance clients, not only the skills.
3. Get an internship (real work experience) in month 5-6, which shows on a resume.
4. Get job assistance when you are ready: resume, interview preparation and hiring partners, after your portfolio is built.

The 3-Month course is a good start (see `digital-marketing-3-months.md`); it does not include the Freelancing Launchpad or the internship. Choose 3 Months if 6 does not fit your time or budget, with honesty.

- Portfolio: practice page, SEO audit, content calendar, 2 live client projects, analytics report, brand strategy, e-commerce plan, YouTube channel
- Certificates to aim for: Divy Digital Hub completion certificate; free Google Skillshop, Meta Blueprint and HubSpot certificates
- Freelancing: Freelancing Launchpad in month 5
- Internship + job assistance in months 5-6
