# Course Catalogue: Divy Digital Hub

Source: the courses page on divydigitalhub.com (screenshot shared by the owner, Oct 2026). All fees are **+ taxes**.
The admissions-counselor quotes only what is written here.

## 🎯 Promotion priority (owner's instruction)
1. **Digital Marketing, 6 Months (₹35,000)**: the main course to promote. The owner's view: this is the course where a student learns properly, with internship, placement and a freelancing launchpad.
2. **Digital Marketing, 3 Months (₹20,000)**: the second option, when 6 months genuinely doesn't fit the student's time or budget. Always present 6 months first, then 3 months.
3. **Digital Marketing, 45 Days (₹9,990)**: don't promote it. Mention it only if the student asks for it directly, or neither of the longer options is possible. Explain honestly what it does not cover (advanced SEO/SEM, live client projects, placement).
4. App Development and Website Design: offer them only when the student's goal is coding or building apps/websites.

## What every course includes (owner, Oct 2026)
- **Basic to advanced:** every course starts from zero and goes to an advanced level.
- **AI tools:** students learn to use AI tools for content, design, ads and productivity.
- **YouTube:** creating, growing and using a YouTube channel.
- **100% job assistance:** resume building, interview preparation and access to hiring partners for every eligible student. This is **assistance, not a guaranteed job**. Always say it that way.

## Batches, fees & offers
- **Batch timings:** 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM, or 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM. The student picks the one that suits them.
- **Mode:** **offline only**, at the Ladwa centre (Gali No. 1, behind Bus Stand). No online classes. Tell students from nearby towns (Kurukshetra, Yamunanagar, Pipli, Radaur, Shahabad, etc.) about the travel up front.
- **Installments:** every course can be paid in **3 equal installments** (taxes extra on each).
  - **6-month courses:** 1st at joining, 2nd at the start of month 2, 3rd at the start of month 4 (confirmed by the owner).
  - **3-month, 4-month and 45-day courses:** these end before month 4, so their due dates still need the owner's confirmation. [Suggested: 3-month/4-month: joining, month 2, month 3 · 45-day: joining, day 15, day 30.] Until it's confirmed, the counselor says "3 installments; we'll confirm the dates on call".
