Owner: Admissions Counselor | Task T-002 | Status: DRAFT for owner approval

## 1. Goal
Run free 45-60 minute seminars at colleges, ITIs, polytechnics and senior secondary schools in Ladwa, Kurukshetra, Pipli, Shahabad, Radaur, Yamunanagar and Babain. The goal is awareness and opt-in sign-ups for the free demo class and free counselling. It is not a hard sell on the stage.

Rules for every seminar:
- Only with the written or verbal permission of the principal / TPO / HOD (see `principal-letter.md`).
- Collect contacts only through the opt-in sign-up form below. Never copy a college's student list or collect numbers without the student's own consent.
- Under-18 students (mostly school classes 11-12): no fee or enrollment talk with the student. Collect only name, class, town and a parent's contact, and only with the school's permission. Parent consent is confirmed by phone before any marketing message.
- Say "100% job assistance" (resume, interview prep, hiring partner access). Never say it is a job promise. No salary figures, no "limited seats", no made-up deadlines.
- Numbers like "500+ students trained" and "50+ hiring partners": say "as per our records".

## 4. Live demo idea with AI tools (10-15 minutes, uses a free AI chatbot and a free design tool)
"Ek local dukaan ka poora social media kit, 10 minute mein."
1. Ask the audience to pick a local business type (e.g. mithai ki dukaan, tuition centre, salon).
2. Prompt the AI chatbot on screen: "Write 5 Instagram captions in Hinglish for a sweet shop in Ladwa for Diwali, friendly tone." Show the output and then edit it live (point: AI is a helper, you add judgment).
3. Ask the AI for a 30-second reel script (hook, 3 scenes, call to action) and a list of 10 hashtags.
4. Make a simple poster in a free design tool using the AI caption.
5. Ask the AI: "Give 10 Google Search keywords a customer in Ladwa would type to find this shop." Explain how this becomes SEO / Google Ads.
6. Close: "Yeh sab hum course mein basic se advanced tak sikhate hain, real business ke saath."
Use only your own demo business or an imaginary one; never show a real client's private data. Check college Wi-Fi/projector before the day and keep a screen recording as backup.
