# Lead Ads Plan: Meta Lead Form + Google Search (20-30 km around Ladwa)

## NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL - do not launch
No ad account spend, boosting or bulk message may start until the owner approves the budget, the creatives and the lead form in writing. Everything below is a draft.

Facts: `work/agency-profile.md` and `work/training/courses.md` only. Offer in ads: free demo class and free counselling. Do not put discount amounts in ads unless the owner wants it (the two discounts are in `courses.md`; "ask in counselling" is safer).

## 1. Objective
- Campaign goal: leads (registrations for the free demo class / free counselling call), not direct sales.
- Success measures: cost per lead, % who answer the call, demo-class attendance, counselling done, enrollments. Track in `weekly-report-template.md`.
- Because classes are offline only at Ladwa, the radius is limited to places reachable daily.

## 2. Geography
- 20-30 km radius around Ladwa. Suggested start: 25 km radius pin on Ladwa. This covers Pipli, Kurukshetra, Shahabad, Babain and nearby parts of Yamunanagar district (Radaur side) approximately; check the reach in Ads Manager and trim towns that are too far for a daily commute.
- All creative says "Offline classes at Ladwa" so nobody expects online classes.

## 3. Audiences (Meta)
- Location: the radius above (people living in or recently in the area).
- Age: 18-45 for the lead-form campaign. Reason: the owner asked for 17+, but Meta lead forms collect personal data, and under-18s need parent consent (India's DPDP Act). 17-year-olds and school students should be reached through parents (age 25-50 audience with the "for your child" creative) or at seminars where the parent's contact is taken with consent. **Owner to confirm.**
- Targeting method: broad targeting with location and age only, letting Meta's delivery find people, plus interest signals such as "Digital marketing", "Social media marketing", "Online marketing", "Computer course". Meta restricts sensitive targeting and Advantage+ audience applies; do not target or exclude by religion, caste, health, financial status, marital status, or any personal attribute, and do not write ad text that assumes it (see section 7).
- Custom audiences: only from people who opted in (lead-form openers, website visitors via the Meta pixel with the cookie banner, demo-class registrants who consented). Never upload purchased or consent-less lists.
- Delivery spots: Instagram feed/reels/stories, Facebook feed/reels (automatic selection is fine).
- Optimization: leads (instant form, "higher intent" form type with a review step).

## 5. Lead form (Meta instant form)
Form type: Higher intent (with review). Intro: "Free demo class aur counselling ke liye register karein."
Questions:
1. Full name (prefilled)
2. Phone number (prefilled)
3. Town (short answer) - used to check distance
4. Aap abhi kya kar rahe hain? (Student / Graduate / Job / Own business / Homemaker)
5. Aapka goal? (Job / Freelancing / Apna business badhana / Side income / Skill upgrade)
6. Kaunsa slot suit karega? (11 AM-1 PM / 4 PM-6 PM)
7. Kya aap 18 saal ya usse zyada ke hain? (Haan / Nahi, parent ke saath baat karunga) - if No, we call the parent only.
Privacy policy link: the website privacy page (to be published; required by Meta).
Custom disclaimer (consent): "Submit karke aap Divy Digital Hub ko call/WhatsApp se demo class aur course ki jaankari dene ki ijazat dete hain. Aap kabhi bhi STOP likh kar mana kar sakte hain."
Thank-you screen: "Dhanyavaad! Hum 1 din ke andar call karenge. Ya abhi call karein: +91 97794 09669." (Do not promise a faster time than the team can keep.)
Process: leads go to `students.csv` with source "Meta lead ad"; first call within the same day; honour "Do not contact" instantly.

## 7. Policy checklist (Meta and Google education ads)
- No assured jobs, income or results: ads say "100% job assistance" only, and the landing page explains it as resume, interview prep and access to hiring partners.
- No claims about being "top / No. 1 / best" without proof; no fake reviews or invented numbers. If using "500+ students trained", add "as per our records" on the landing page.
- No sensitive personal-attribute assumptions in text or targeting; no targeting by minors' interests; no ads aimed at under-18s for lead capture.
- Accurate pricing only: if fees appear, use the exact `courses.md` figures with "+ taxes" (Digital Marketing 6 Months Rs 35,000 + taxes; Digital Marketing 3 Months Rs 20,000 + taxes).
- Landing page must show the address, phone, privacy policy and course details clearly (Google's destination requirements).
- Meta Special Ad Category: not required for education courses, but confirm in Ads Manager and follow any prompt.
- Respect the opt-out, honour `Do not contact`.
