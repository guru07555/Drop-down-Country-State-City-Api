# Free Demo Class Plan: "Digital Marketing se Pehla Client Kaise Laayein"

Status: DRAFT. **Suggestion for the owner to confirm:** a recurring free demo class once a week on a weekday (the office is open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM, so not Sunday), Suggested: every Friday, 2:00–3:00 PM, between the two class slots. The owner confirms the day and time. Offline only, at the Ladwa centre (Gali No. 1, behind Bus Stand). The demo class and counselling are free, as stated in `courses.md`.

Facts used: `work/agency-profile.md`, `work/training/courses.md`. No seat limits, no deadlines and no invented offers are mentioned.

## 1. Format
- Duration: about 90 minutes (60 min session + 30 min counselling slots).
- Who attends: only people who registered (form, call, WhatsApp, walk-in, seminar sign-up). Walk-ins are welcome and are registered on the spot.
- Under-18: must come with a parent/guardian or have a parent's consent recorded on the form. Fee or enrollment discussion is with the parent only.
- Nearby-town attendees: tell them in the reminder that classes are offline at Ladwa and how to reach (bus stand, Gali No. 1 behind it).

## 2. Agenda (60 minutes + counselling)
| Min | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Welcome, attendee intro round (name, town, goal) |
| 5-15 | Digital marketing in 10 minutes: SEO, Google Maps, Ads, social media, email, analytics, with local business examples |
| 15-30 | Live AI demo: create a month of content ideas, captions, a poster and a reel script for a local business; find keywords with an AI tool |
| 30-40 | "Pehla client kaise laayein": audit a local shop's Google Maps and Instagram, a one-page offer, how to pitch politely |
| 40-50 | How our program works: Digital Marketing 6 Months first, then 3 Months (what is in each, live projects, YouTube, 100% job assistance, internship and freelancing launchpad in the 6-month plan). Fees, 3 installments and the discounts exactly as in `courses.md` |
| 50-60 | Q&A |
| 60-90 | One-to-one free counselling (5-10 min each): goal, level, time, laptop, budget, decision-maker (for under 18, parent) |

Tell attendees: "Aaj enroll karna zaroori nahi hai. Aap sochkar, parents se baat karke decide kar sakte hain."

## 5. Moving attendees to counselling and enrollment
1. At the class, mark attendance and tick "counselling interested".
2. Same day: 5-10 minute counselling with each interested person. Ask goal, level, time, mode, laptop, budget, decision-maker. Recommend Digital Marketing 6 Months first; offer the 3 Months only when time or budget genuinely does not fit; offer Website Design or App Development only for a coding / website / app goal.
3. Record in `work/training/students.csv`; status to `Counselled` then `Demo attended`.
4. Within 24 hours send the personal plan (`work/training/plans/<student-id>-<name>.md`) and the fee details exactly as in `courses.md`, including 3 equal installments.
5. Objections: fees -> installments, or the 3-month plan; "YouTube se seekh lunga" -> mentorship, live projects, feedback and real agency work; time -> choose either daily slot; distance -> be honest that classes are offline at Ladwa only.
6. Discounts are only the two in `courses.md` (full payment upfront on DM 6 Months, and Rs 1,000 off for advance booking 7+ days before joining). They can never be combined (owner confirmed). Any other discount needs the owner's approval.
7. On enrollment: status `Enrolled`, add to the batch list, ask `account-manager` for the receipt.
